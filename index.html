About Me

I am a researcher in Fluid Mechanics, focusing on the development of high-fidelity sharp-interface methods and their applications in binary solidification/melting and surfactant-laden flows. I received my Ph.D. from Xi’an Jiaotong University (XJTU) in January 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Jie Zhang. Starting in February 2027, I will join IMFT in France as an AToUT Fellow.
